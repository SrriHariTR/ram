Frequently asked questions

Manufacturing Systems & Industrial AI FAQs

What is MES/MOM?

Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) and Manufacturing Operations Management (MOM) support the coordination, monitoring and management of manufacturing operations. Their scope depends on the production environment and business requirements.

How can AI help manufacturing operations?

Depending on data availability and use-case suitability, AI can help analyze quality variation, process deviations, energy consumption, equipment conditions and technical documentation.

Why is industrial data integration important?

Integration can help make relevant information from shop-floor and enterprise systems available for reporting, analytics and AI applications, subject to system compatibility and appropriate controls.

What does MES support involve?

Depending on the service agreement, MES support may include troubleshooting, incident management, application maintenance, interface monitoring and continuous improvement.
